
>[!info] Core Mechanics
>- Cook Food | Goal is to cook multiple foods at once
>- Time Based Cooking | Cooking the food takes time to cook but that time speeds up when not looking at the food
>- Fail condition | Foods can overcook 

>[!info] Functional Mechanics
>- Camera Switch between foods
>- Foods detect observation to adapt their cook time
>- Score increases based on how fast foods are cooked
>- As level increases, amount of foods to manage also increase
